Why it exists

You know you spent it. You just can’t see where.

I once paid ₹4,000 for sandals I never wear, and bought a ₹150 protein bar four or five times a week without seeing what that added up to. Every tracking app I tried failed one of two ways.

  • Typing every paymentIt works for a week, then the habit is gone.
  • Linking your bank accountMany people don't want to hand over that access.
  • No link to what you wantA list of spends doesn't show what they cost you.

How it works

Pay as usual. AutoFlow does the rest.

  1. You pay by UPI or cardYour bank sends its usual SMS.
  2. AutoFlow captures it immediatelyIt reads the SMS on your phone and saves the amount and the shop as an expense. You type nothing.
  3. A small notification confirms the captureIt shows the amount and the shop, so you know it was recorded.
  4. Fix it from the notificationWrong or missing category? Tap to change it. Not a real expense? Tap “Not an expense” and it won't count. You don't need to open the app. Payments AutoFlow isn't sure about go to the Review tab. They already count in your total and wait for you to confirm.
  5. Open the app any timeSee what you've spent this month and where it went: food, travel, bills and more.

Goals

Save with intent, then spend the rest without guilt.

Pick something you want, like a PS5 or a pair of running shoes. When you skip a purchase, add a “Saved” entry and say how you saved. It moves the progress bar.

  • Goals are only a way to keep track.
  • They never touch your spending or your real money.
  • A deadline is optional.

On your home screen

Add an entry in one tap.

The widgets show this month's total and two buttons, + Expense and + Saved, so a cash spend or a saving takes seconds. In the evening, a daily recap sums up the day.

Privacy first

Built privacy first. Your spending never leaves your phone.

The app has no internet access at all, so there is nowhere for your data to go. Your messages and your spending stay on your phone. There is no login, no ads and no tracking.

  • Your data never leaves your phone, because the app can't connect to the internet.
  • No bank account to link.
  • It reads only incoming bank alerts and never saves the message text.
  • You can turn the notifications and the daily recap on or off in Settings.
  • The code is public, so you can read exactly what it does: AutoFlow on GitHub.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does AutoFlow need the internet?

No. The app has no internet access at all, so your spending and your messages stay on your phone.

Is my financial data safe? Who can see it?

Only you. Your spending stays on your phone, and the app has no internet access, so there is no way for it to send your data anywhere. There is no account or login, so there is no server holding your information. There are no ads and no tracking, and the code is public if you want to check.

What does it read from my SMS?

It reads only incoming bank alerts, to pick out the amount and the shop. It never saves the message text.

Do I need to sign up or link my bank account?

No. There is no login and no bank account to link. Install it, allow SMS access, and your payments start appearing.

Why does Android show warnings when I install it?

AutoFlow installs from a file instead of the Play Store, so Android adds a few safety prompts. They are normal for apps installed this way. The pictures in the install section show what to tap at each one, and a Google Play version is coming soon.

What if a payment is wrong or isn't really an expense?

Tap “Change category” or “Not an expense” on the notification, or edit the payment inside the app. Payments AutoFlow isn't sure about wait in the Review tab, and they already count in your total until you decide.

What about cash payments?

Add them with Quick expense in the app, or with the “+ Expense” widget on your home screen. Both take a few seconds.

What are Goals? Does AutoFlow move my money?

Never. A goal is a motivation jar: you pick something you want and record what you save towards it. AutoFlow cannot move or touch your real money.

What is the Autopay page?

It is for automatic payments like subscriptions. These are normal expenses and already count in your totals. The page is new, so it may be empty at first.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not right now. AutoFlow is an Android app.

Is it free? Can I check what the app does?

It is free, with no ads. The code is public, so you can read exactly what it does on GitHub.
